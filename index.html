Our Heritage

Rooted in Ugandan Soil

At Sanyuka Decor Florists, we believe that flowers are nature’s poetry. Uganda’s unique equatorial climate, rich volcanic earth, and high altitudes yield some of the most vibrant, resilient, and fragrant flowers in the world.

We partner directly with family-run farms in Fort Portal, Mt. Elgon, and Entebbe, ensuring our growers are paid fair wages while bringing you blossoms at the peak of their freshness. From corporate styling in Nakasero to intimate bridal bouquets in Muyenga, we craft statements of luxury and organic beauty.
